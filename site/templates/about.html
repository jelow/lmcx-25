Lower Mainland Cyclocross is a grassroots cycling organization committed to building an inclusive, accessible, and exciting cyclocross community across the Vancouver area. Our mission is to spark enthusiasm for the sport by supporting riders of all ages and skill levels, uniting local race organizers, and promoting events through clear, centralized communication.

What is Cyclocross?
Cyclocross races are characterized by mixed terrain (grass, mud, sand…) and are typically held in the fall season. Races are short and punchy, lasting between 30-60 minutes, where riders navigate changing surface types, tight turns and various obstacles (barriers, stairs) along the course.
Why try Racing?
Community is a big part of what makes cyclocross racing so fun! Yes, some racers are competitive but many are here to simply play around on bikes, try something new and (lovingly) heckle their fellow riders. Since racers complete multiple laps of a short course, events are spectator-friendly and the perfect opportunity to make a new friend before or after your category.
Cyclocross is guaranteed to level up your bike handling skills as you’ll be practicing keeping control of the bike on bumpy or slippery surfaces. You’ll be able to carry these skills over into your future riding, whether that’s on the road or trails.
Every event features multiple race categories based on ability level and/or age, so there’s always someone to compete with. But you don’t need a competitive attitude to have a good time! Just show up, ride your bike, and maybe grab a beer with us at the LMCX tent!
What do I need to get started?

  1. Bike: Although there are cyclocross-specific bikes, you don’t need anything super specialized right away. Any bike that you’re comfortable riding on grass, gravel or mud will work just fine. If you’re racing in our Launch category, a hybrid bike, commuter or even a mountain bike is enough to get you started. As long as it doesn’t have a motor, it’s allowed! It is highly recommended to use tires that are at least 700x33 wide, and you’ll want some tread on them. Remember that a heavier bike will be more challenging to lift over obstacles, so you’ll want to remove any pannier racks, fenders or other accessories before race day.
  2. Shoes: Since cyclocross typically involves some running, road cycling shoes with the bulky 3-bolt cleats aren’t recommended. Mountain bike shoes with the smaller SPD-style cleats are ideal. If you don’t have those, running shoes and flat pedals work great too.
  3. Helmet: Do we have to explain this one?
  4. Confidence: We’ll be hosting some practice sessions throughout the summer and early fall. We’d love to help you learn some basic cross skills and set you up for success in your first race. Check out our Events page or follow us on Instagram to know more.

KinderCross is back for another season! This is a free activity, geared towards riders ages 2-8 and runs alongside our main race series. KinderCross participants get the opportunity to race on a fully separate kiddie course, or on a shortened section of the main course. This is an initiative designed to introduce young riders—especially those from underrepresented age groups and communities—to the joy of cyclocross in a fun, welcoming environment. By fostering connection, participation, and diversity within the sport, Lower Mainland Cyclocross aims to make cycling more than just a race—it’s a community.

Older kids are encouraged to race on the main course, either in the Launch group or in their appropriate age category: U13, U15, U17 or U19. Thanks to a generous grant from Cycling Canada, our youth racers will race free all season in 2026!

We encourage you to come to a Wednesday night practice to learn more, or attend a cyclocross clinic, or just sign up and show up! Then hang around, and cheer on your friends or kids at the KinderCross race.
