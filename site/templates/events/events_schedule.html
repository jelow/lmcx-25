Race Categories & Wave Guide:

LMCX races are self-seeding, meaning it’s your call. Choose the category where you have people to chase and folks to chase you. No sandbagging!

  • Launch Wave - 10:30 AM (30 min)

    • This is the wave for new racers, those who are just getting into cyclocross, and those who want to have fun and learn. This wave is also for kids U13 and U15, as well as singlespeed and tracklocross riders.

    • Non-Binary (New for 2026!): We are thrilled to introduce a dedicated Non-Binary category this year to champion inclusivity, gender diversity, and vibrant racing in cyclocross. This open-ability category is for non-binary, gender-queer, agender, and gender-transcendent athletes who want a welcoming, competitive space on the course. If numbers are small, ya’ll will have the option of joining the start of the Open race so you have more folks to compete with!
    • Launch (Formerly Beginner or Novice) / U13 / U15 (Open & Women): Perfect for anyone brand new to bicycle racing or cyclocross—whether you're young, old, or anywhere in between! (If you're too fast, we'll give you a gentle nudge up for next time). U13/U15 receive separate series points, but share race-day podiums with their Launch wave.
    • Singlespeed & Tracklocross: One gear, no excuses. A fun, spirited category for fixed-gear (with or without brakes) and single-speed setups.

  • Intermediate Wave - 11:15 AM (45 min)

    • For racers stepping up from the Launch wave who are getting faster, building stamina, and ready for a longer 45-minute effort.

    • Intermediate / U17 (Open & Women): Graduated from Launch and building speed, but not quite ready for the Advanced/Elite field? This is your category. U17 riders are scored separately for series points but will have a single race-day podium with the rest of the intermediate riders.
    • Masters Women (40+): Designed for experienced women riders aged 40 and up looking for a competitive 45-minute battle in a dedicated field. We will start the Masters Women with the intermediates to support a larger field.

  • Intermission & Kids Races - 12:15 PM

    • Take a break, grab some food, cheer on the little ones, or take a spin!

    • Party Lap / Intro Ride (12:15 PM): One fun, casual lap on the main course. All bike types, costumes, and skill levels welcome! Cargo, tall, tandem, unicycle, buddy-bike, you name it.
    • Younglings (12:17 PM): A 1-lap "taster" race on the main course for kids aged 9+ who are ready for a taste of real cross racing before stepping up to Launch/U13.
    • KinderCross (12:20 PM – 12:30 PM): Short, mini-course races broken down by age (2–4, 5–6, and 7–8). Run bikes, pedal bikes, training wheels, and gears are all welcome! (Drop-in coaching & registration opens at 11:00 AM).
    • Course Open Practice (12:30 PM) & Podiums (12:45 PM): Practice the course, watch out for any kinders who may be finishing up their laps please.

  • Masters Wave - 1:15 PM (45 min)

    • Fast, tactical 45-minute racing for seasoned veteran riders.

    • Masters 40-54 (Open): For folks aged 40 to 54 who have moved past the beginner/intermediate stage and are looking for high-tempo racing without entering the Elite wave.
    • Super Masters 55+ (Open): A dedicated category for experienced riders 55 and older who want to race against their peers on a full 45-minute clock.

  • Elite Wave - 2:15 PM (50–60 min)

    • High-speed, high-intensity racing featuring the fastest riders on the circuit.

    • Advanced / U19 (Open & Women): (Formerly Expert) Open to any age. If you’ve raced Pro, Expert, Cat 1/2, or land in the top half of a Cat 3 field, step up to the plate. Expect full-gas racing for 50 minutes (Women+) to 60 minutes (Open).
